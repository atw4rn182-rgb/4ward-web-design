4Ward Web Design, LLC — Website Service Agreement

Effective version: service-agreement-v1 · Carlsbad, New Mexico

This Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is between 4Ward Web Design, LLC (“Provider”) and the client identified during onboarding (“Client”).

1. Services

Provider will design, develop, host, and maintain a website according to the Client’s selected tier, as described for that tier on the pricing page. Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 include Google Business Profile setup. Tier 4 — Online Flyer does not. Hosting and routine maintenance continue while a monthly subscription remains active, within that tier’s update allowance.

2. Fees & billing

A one-time $200 Launch Fee applies to all tiers and covers initial consultation, custom design/setup, domain/DNS if needed, basic on-page SEO, Google Analytics, contact forms where the tier includes them, mobile optimization, testing, and onboarding. A standard domain registration is included only where the selected tier says so. Premium and aftermarket domains are not included. Client also authorizes recurring monthly charges for the selected subscription, or one upfront buyout payment of 24 months plus the launch fee. Monthly billing does not end automatically after 24 months. Fees are billed through Stripe. Optional recurring website add-ons selected during onboarding are added to the monthly subscription shown before payment. Tier 2 includes the private admin dashboard in its price. The dashboard remains an optional $50/month add-on on Tier 1 and Tier 3. It is not included with Tier 4. The dashboard is a private back-office for the business. It is not a public page, and it requires setup and ongoing maintenance. One-time or custom services (logo, print, chatbot, extra pages, extra updates) are quoted separately and billed with a Stripe payment link. Extra updates beyond the included monthly allowance that are not purchased as a quote are billed separately at the rates listed for that tier. English and Spanish, when requested, are included at no extra charge.

3. Client responsibilities

Client will provide accurate business information, logos, photos, copy, and timely feedback. Delays in Client materials may extend delivery timelines.

4. Intellectual property

Until all amounts due are paid, Provider retains rights in unfinished work product. After payment of amounts due under a buy-out, ownership of the delivered website design/content created specifically for Client transfers as described for that package. Subscription clients receive a license to use the live site while their account remains in good standing.

5. Term & cancellation

Monthly subscriptions continue until canceled. They do not end automatically after 24 months. Client may cancel future billing through Provider with notice before the next renewal. The $200 launch fee is nonrefundable. The first monthly subscription payment may be refunded within 14 days of the original purchase if the client changes their mind. That 14-day period starts at purchase, not at website preview or completion. It does not apply to an upfront buyout. Later monthly payments that have already been processed are nonrefundable except where required by law. An upfront buyout pays 24 months plus the launch fee at once and transfers ownership of the delivered website as described above. Later hosting, domain renewal, support, and content updates are not included unless arranged separately.

6. Limitation of liability

Services are provided professionally and in good faith. Provider is not liable for indirect or consequential damages. Total liability under this Agreement is limited to fees paid by Client to Provider in the three (3) months preceding a claim.

7. Acceptance

By typing their name below and checking the acceptance box, Client agrees to these terms electronically with the same effect as a handwritten signature.
